Editing the config does not restart the pod. kubelet needs up to about a minute
to project the change into the volume, and the page then picks it up on its next
ETag poll (every 30s, unless you set reload.interval in the config).

Two things stop that working, and neither is used by this chart: a ConfigMap
mounted with subPath is never updated by kubelet, and an immutable ConfigMap
cannot change at all. If you adapt these manifests, do not reintroduce either.
